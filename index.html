📋 SLP Instructor Preparation Guide: Intended Use & Session Flow

Designed for elementary school-based sessions (1:1 or small groups of 2–4 students) targeting functional communication, self-regulation, social inferencing, and problem-solving.

Step 1 • Setup 💻

1. Choose Your Screen Layout

  • Clinician-Guided View (Default): Keep 👩‍🏫 SLP Prompts: ON in the top bar so you have 4 targeted question types right beside each scene.
  • Full-Stage Student View: Click 👩‍🏫 SLP Prompts to toggle the purple drawer off when projecting on a smartboard or letting students drive the mouse.
  • Audio Control: Keep 🔊 Sound ON for synthesized Scottie barks, Piggy squeaks, and read-alouds, or mute it if you prefer reading live with dramatic pauses.
Step 2 • Multimodal 💬

2. Set Up the Core Word AAC Strip

  • Pinned Bottom Bar: The 14-tile core vocabulary strip (GO, WAIT, STOP, HELP, LOOK, WANT, BREATHE, FEEL, UH-OH, JOVI, PIGGY) stays visible across every tab.
  • Aided Language Modeling: Tap 2–3 core tiles as you talk (e.g., JOVI + LOOK + WAIT) and press 🔊 Speak Strip to model multimodal sentence building.
Step 3 • Data Collection 📊

3. Track Trials in Real Time

  • Zero Paper Tallying: At the bottom of the purple SLP drawer, tap ✅ Indep., 💡 Cued, or 🤝 Modeled after each student turn.
  • End-of-Session Export: Open the 📊 SLP Session Data tab at the end of the session, type student initials, and click 📋 Copy Progress Note for an instant SOAP summary.

⏱️ Recommended 25–30 Minute Therapy Session Flow (The “Predict → Contrast → Regulate” Cycle)

Works for 1:1 or Small Groups (2–4 Kids)
Minutes 0–4 • Warm-Up

1. Meet Jovi & Piggy (Spread 1)

Introduce Jovi (a real 15-lb black Scottie in Omaha!) and her pink stuffed Piggy. Ask students what Jovi wants RIGHT NOW (breakfast, walkies, belly rubs) and connect to things students hate waiting for at school.

Minutes 4–16 • Core Story

2. Predict Before Clicking (Spreads 2–12)

On each spread, pause before clicking! Ask the 🔮 Predicting question first. Then let students click ⚡ Paws Say GO! to observe the funny consequence (UH-OH!), followed by 🧠 Brain Says WAIT! to contrast outcomes.

Minutes 16–23 • Carryover

3. Active Skill Lab (Tabs 2 & 3)

Jump to 🌬️ Breath Coach to practice the 3-step routine, 🍲 Jovi's Bowl to describe her 8 homemade dinner ingredients, or 🪟 Window Patrol to match Jovi's Alert Level (1, 2, or 3) to Hickory Street clues.

Minutes 23–28 • Wrap-Up

4. Student Teaches Piggy (Spread 12)

On Spread 12, flip roles: have the student become the expert and coach Piggy through “Pause your paws. Take a breath. Choose what's next.” Export your session note in 1 click!

🎯 How to Differentiate Across Your Elementary Caseload (K–5th Grade)

Student Profile / Grade Band Best Spreads & Tabs to Use Target Skills & Prompts Multimodal / Scaffold Strategy
Early Elementary (K–1st) & Multimodal / AAC Communicators Spreads 1–4, Spread 7 (Snack), & 🍲 Jovi's Homemade Bowl Core vocabulary (GO, WAIT, STOP, UH-OH, MORE), basic cause/effect, and food/color/category describing Use the bottom AAC bar to build 2-word phrases (JOVI GO, PIGGY LOOK) before clicking the big scene buttons
Mid-Elementary (2nd–3rd) • Functional Requesting & Self-Regulation Spreads 5–8 & 🌬️ Breath Coach Replacing impulsive actions with functional self-advocacy (Spread 8: nudging Dad's knee to ask for help), labeling emotions, and reframing mistakes (Spread 5) Contrast the ⚡ GO outcome with the 🧠 WAIT outcome on every page; have students role-play how to ask for help in class
Upper Elementary (4th–5th) • Social Inferencing & Executive Function Spreads 8–12 & 🪟 Hickory Street Window Patrol Reading subtle environmental clues (Spread 10: visitor's hands are full of a coat and bag), growth mindset (Spread 9: stopping one paw), and matching reaction size to problem size Toggle SLP Prompts: OFF so students analyze the visual clues independently first, then justify their prediction using “Because...” clauses
