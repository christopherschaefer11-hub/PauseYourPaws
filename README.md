# Pause Your Paws: Jovi & Piggy's Interactive SLP Companion 🐾🐷

An interactive, single-file web application based on the 12-spread children's story **"Jovi and the Very Big WAIT"**, custom-built for **Shaila Schaefer's** elementary Speech-Language Pathology (SLP) sessions at Bellevue Public Schools.

---

## ✨ What's Inside

1. **📖 Interactive 12-Spread Story Mode (*Jovi and the Very Big WAIT*)**
   - Follows **Jovi** (your 15-lb black Scottish Terrier in Omaha) and her beloved pink stuffed pig **Piggy** through all 12 scenes of your story:
     1. *Meet Jovi & Piggy in Omaha* ("Waiting seemed like a terrible waste of dog.")
     2. *The Fence Squirrel & Watering Tray* (*SPLOOSH!* while Piggy watches from the patio chair beside his tiny sunhat)
     3. *Kitchen Aromas & The Dish Towel Skid* (*SKIIIIID!* "Her bottom arrived before her manners.")
     4. *DING-DONG! The Sock Basket Welcome* (One sock on Jovi's head, two mismatched socks on Piggy's ears!)
     5. *Big Feelings Beside Piggy* ("Big feelings don't make you bad, Jovi. You're learning. Waiting takes practice.")
     6. *Pause, Breathe, Choose Practice Rug* ("Pause your paws. Take a breath. Choose what's next.")
     7. *The Crunchy Snack Test* ("The snack was still delicious. It had not vanished during the waiting.")
     8. *Piggy on the Sofa — Functional Requesting* (Nudging Dad's knee to ask for help so Piggy arrives with both ears attached!)
     9. *The Cartwheeling Autumn Leaf* (Stopping just one front paw—"Sometimes her brain said, 'A little.'")
     10. *The Visitor Returns with a Squeaky Ball* (Spotting the social clue that the visitor's hands are full of a coat and bag)
     11. *Broom-Tail Wag & Room for Both* (Sitting for 2 seconds makes space for a gentle scratch AND fetch!)
     12. *Bedtime Coaching for Piggy* ("Your turn to practice, Piggy. Honestly. Such big feelings.")
   - Every spread lets students test **"⚡ Paws Say GO!"** (impulse outcome) vs. **"🧠 Brain Says WAIT!"** (regulated outcome) with dynamic custom SVG animations and Web Audio sound effects (Scottie barks, Piggy squeaks, water splashes, and chimes).

2. **👩‍🏫 Shaila's Clinical Companion Drawer (Toggleable)**
   - Built-in prompts tailored to each of the 12 spreads for:
     - **Predicting & Problem-Solving** (*"What happens if..."*)
     - **Perspective-Taking & Feelings**
     - **Functional Expression & Self-Advocacy**
     - **Real-Life Classroom Connections**
   - Includes a 1-click **Trial Logger** (`Independent`, `Cued`, `Modeled`) right in the sidebar.

3. **💬 Multimodal Core Word AAC Strip (Pinned & Toggleable)**
   - Color-coded core vocabulary tiles (`GO`, `WAIT`, `STOP`, `HELP`, `LOOK`, `WANT`, `BREATHE`, `FEEL`, `UH-OH`, `MORE`, `MY TURN`, `ALL DONE`, `JOVI`, `PIGGY`) that build an interactive sentence strip and speak aloud using the browser's native Web Speech API (`speechSynthesis`).

4. **🌬️ "Pause, Breathe, Choose" Self-Regulation Station**
   - Standalone 3-step visual regulation tool (`1. Pause Your Paws` -> `2. Sniff In & Puff Out` -> `3. Choose What's Next`) with an animated Scottie breathing pacer (3s or 5s).

5. **🍲 Jovi's Homemade Bowl & 🪟 Hickory Street Window Patrol**
   - **Jovi's Real Homemade Dinner Bowl**: Describing & sequencing game using Jovi's actual recipe (dry kibble, boiled chicken breast, jasmine rice, green beans, lima beans, sweet peas, carrots, and corn) plus a button to toss a bonus treat to her older brother **Snoop**!
   - **Hickory Street Window Patrol**: Social inferencing & "Size of the Problem" game where students help Jovi choose between *Level 1 (Calm Look)*, *Level 2 (One Polite Woof)*, or *Level 3 (Hurl All 15 Pounds at the Window!)* when a blue sedan passes, a package arrives, or Snoop naps on the deck.

6. **📊 SLP Session Data & SOAP Progress Note Generator**
   - Automatically calculates accuracy/independence percentages and formats a copy-pasteable clinical progress summary.

## 🚀 How to Use on Shaila's Computer or GitHub Pages

### Option 1: Open Directly on Shaila's Computer (Offline-Ready)
1. Save `index.html` to Shaila's desktop or laptop.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari.
3. Bookmark it in her browser bar for 1-click access during sessions!

### Option 2: Deploy Free on GitHub Pages (Shareable Link)
1. Create a new repository on GitHub (e.g., `jovi-pause-your-paws`).
2. Add `index.html` and `README.md` to the repository and commit to `main`.
3. In your GitHub repo, go to **Settings** -> **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` / `/ (root)`, and click **Save**.
5. In ~60 seconds, your live web app will be ready at `https://<your-username>.github.io/jovi-pause-your-paws/`!
