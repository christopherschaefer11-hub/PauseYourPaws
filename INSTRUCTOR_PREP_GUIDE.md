# 📋 SLP Instructor Preparation Guide: "Pause Your Paws" (Jovi & Piggy)

**Clinical Companion to *Jovi and the Very Big WAIT***  
**Designed for:** Elementary Speech-Language Pathology (K–5th Grade, 1:1 or Small Groups of 2–4)  
**Primary Clinical Domains:** Social Problem-Solving, Emotional Regulation, Perspective-Taking & Social Inferencing, Functional Self-Advocacy, and Multimodal Core Vocabulary (AAC).

---

## 1. Quick 2-Minute Pre-Session Setup Checklist

1. **Launch the App (`index.html`)**
   - Open `index.html` in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari (either directly from your desktop or via your GitHub Pages bookmark).
2. **Choose Your Display Mode (Top-Right Bar)**
   - **👩‍🏫 `SLP Prompts: ON` (Default for 1:1 / Clinician Laptop):** Displays the purple **Shaila's Clinical Companion** drawer beside every story spread with 4 scaffolded prompts (*Predicting*, *Perspective-Taking*, *Functional Advocacy*, and *Student Connection*) plus your 1-click trial counter.
   - **👩‍🏫 `SLP Prompts: OFF` (Best for Smartboards / Group Projection):** Hides the clinician drawer so Jovi and Piggy's animated illustration fills the full width of the screen.
3. **Configure the Multimodal AAC Strip (`💬 AAC Strip: ON/OFF`)**
   - Keep the bottom 14-button core vocabulary bar visible for emerging communicators, multimodal AAC users, or students working on expanding utterances.
   - Test audio by clicking **🔊 Speak Strip** or **🐕‍🦺 Jovi Woof!** (uses native browser Web Speech & Web Audio synthesis—no internet required).

## 2. Recommended 25–30 Minute Therapy Session Flow

### Phase 1: Warm-Up & Character Hook (Minutes 0–4 • Spread 1)
- **Introduce Jovi & Piggy:** Explain that Jovi is a real 15-pound black Scottish Terrier in Omaha who loves her pink stuffed pig (**Piggy**) and believes everything wonderful—breakfast, walkies, and belly rubs—should happen *RIGHT NOW*.
- **Anchor the Refrain:** Read the banner together (or tap `GO` and `WAIT` on the AAC strip):
  > *"My paws say GO. Can my brain say WAIT?"*
- **Student Connection:** Ask each student to name one thing at school or home that makes their "paws say GO" (e.g., recess, iPad turn, cafeteria line).

### Phase 2: The "Predict → Contrast → Regulate" Story Cycle (Minutes 4–16 • Spreads 2–11)
Instead of clicking straight through the story, use this 4-step clinical routine on each spread:
1. **Scan Visual Clues First:** Have students inspect the illustration *before* clicking any choice (e.g., the blue watering tray under the squirrel on Spread 2, the dish towel at the kitchen corner on Spread 3, Dad sitting with a book next to Piggy on Spread 8, or the visitor's hands full of a coat and bag on Spread 10).
2. **Predict the Consequence:** Ask the **🔮 Predicting & Problem-Solving** prompt from the purple drawer (*"What is going to happen if Jovi's paws say GO right now?"*).
3. **Contrast Both Choices (`⚡ Paws Say GO!` vs. `🧠 Brain Says WAIT!`):**
   - Let the student click **⚡ Paws Say GO!** first! Kids love seeing the safe, humorous consequence (splooshing in the watering tray, skidding on the dish towel, or tipping the laundry basket so Piggy gets two mismatched socks on his ears).
   - Use the **🐷 Perspective-Taking & Feelings** prompt to label how Jovi, Piggy, and the family feel.
   - Next, click **🧠 Brain Says WAIT!** (or **🌬️ Take 1 Scottie Breath**) to see how pausing changes the outcome and earns a bone (`+1 🦴`).
4. **Log the Trial:** Tap **✅ Indep.**, **💡 Cued**, or **🤝 Modeled** in the bottom-right corner of the purple drawer.

### Phase 3: Active Skill Generalization (Minutes 16–23 • Choose 1 Lab Tab)
- **For Self-Regulation & Coping Plans → Open `🌬️ Breath Coach`:**
  - Walk students through **Step 1 (Plant Your Paws)**, **Step 2 (Sniff In & Puff Out for 3s or 5s)**, and **Step 3 (Choose What's Next: Ask for help, Wait for a turn, or Take a break)**.
- **For Describing, Categorization & Sequencing → Open `🍲 Jovi's Bowl & Patrol` (Left Panel):**
  - Build Jovi's real homemade dinner bowl! Before clicking each of the 8 ingredients (Dry Kibble, Boiled Chicken, Jasmine Rice, Green Beans, Lima Beans, Sweet Peas, Carrots, Sweet Corn), have the student describe its **category, color, shape, or texture**, then toss a bonus treat to big brother **Snoop**.
- **For Social Inferencing & Size of the Problem → Open `🍲 Jovi's Bowl & Patrol` (Right Panel):**
  - Run **🪟 Jovi's Hickory Street Window Patrol**. Have students read the environmental clues for all 4 scenarios (Passing Blue Sedan, Porch Package, Snoop Napping on the Deck, Visitor with Squeaky Ball) and justify why **Level 1 (Calm Look)** or **Level 2 (One Polite Woof)** fits better than **Level 3 (Hurling All 15 Pounds at the Window)**.

### Phase 4: Role Reversal & Documentation Wrap-Up (Minutes 23–28 • Spread 12 & Data Tab)
- **Student Becomes the Teacher (Spread 12):** At bedtime, Piggy tumbles off the bed. Have the student coach Piggy in their own words through all 3 steps (*"Pause your paws. Take a breath. Choose what's next."*).
- **1-Click Progress Note (`📊 SLP Session Data`):** Enter the student's initials, select the primary clinical goal from the dropdown, add a quick observation, and click **📋 Copy Progress Note** to paste directly into your documentation system.

## 3. Caseload Differentiation Guide (K–5th Grade)

| Caseload Group | Priority Spreads & Modules | Target Language & Clinical Goals | Scaffolding Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **K–1st Grade & Multimodal / AAC Learners** | Spreads 1–4, Spread 7, & **Jovi's Homemade Bowl** | Core words (`GO`, `WAIT`, `STOP`, `HELP`, `LOOK`, `UH-OH`, `MORE`), basic cause/effect, and object description | Model 2-word combinations on the bottom AAC bar (`JOVI GO`, `STOP JOVI`, `WANT HELP`) and press **Speak Strip** |
| **2nd–3rd Grade • Functional Expression & Regulation** | Spreads 5–8 & **Breath Coach** | Replacing physical impulses with functional requests (Spread 8: nudging Dad's knee to ask for Piggy) and growth-mindset self-talk (Spread 5) | Contrast *Unexpected* (`GO`) vs. *Expected* (`WAIT`) outcomes; practice classroom scripts (*"Can you help me, please?"*) |
| **4th–5th Grade • Social Inferencing & Executive Function** | Spreads 8–12 & **Hickory Street Window Patrol** | Context-clue inferencing (Spread 10: full hands), self-monitoring (Spread 9: stopping one paw), and reaction-to-problem scaling | Hide the SLP drawer (`SLP Prompts: OFF`) and challenge students to spot the 2–3 visual clues in each scene before predicting |

## 4. Complete 12-Spread Quick Reference Map

| Spread | Scene Title | Story Event | Primary Clinical Discussion Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Meet Jovi & Piggy in Omaha | Jovi wants breakfast, walkies, and belly rubs *RIGHT NOW* | Why waiting feels hard; choosing *first* vs. demanding everything at once |
| **2** | The Fence Squirrel & Watering Tray | Jovi gallops at a squirrel and lands *SPLOOSH* in the watering tray | Visual scanning & predicting consequences before moving our feet |
| **3** | Kitchen Aromas & The Dish Towel Skid | Jovi hurries nose-first around the corner and *SKIIIIDS* on a dish towel | Expected vs. unexpected speed in shared spaces (kitchen, hallway, cafeteria) |
| **4** | DING-DONG! The Sock Basket Welcome | Jovi delivers 17 greetings at once and bounces into clean socks | Managing excitement during transitions/visitors; cause & effect |
| **5** | Big Feelings Beside Piggy | Jovi whispers *"Am I a bad dog?"*; family reassures her waiting takes practice | Reframing mistakes; distinguishing *having big feelings* from *being bad* |
| **6** | Pause, Breathe, Choose Practice | Jovi plants 4 paws, sniffs in, and puffs out beside upright Piggy | Practicing self-regulation routines when calm so they work when excited |
| **7** | The Crunchy Snack Test | Jovi waits one tiny breath for *"Ready!"* and discovers the snack didn't vanish | Delayed gratification; discovering that waiting doesn't make rewards disappear |
| **8** | Piggy on the Sofa (Asking for Help) | Instead of leaping on Dad's book, Jovi pauses and nudges Dad's knee | **Functional requesting & self-advocacy** when something is out of reach |
| **9** | The Cartwheeling Leaf (*"Good Trying!"*) | Jovi chases the 1st leaf (*Whoops!*), then stops **one front paw** on the 2nd | Growth mindset & partial success (*"A little was something she could practice"*) |
| **10** | Visitor Returns with a Squeaky Ball | A squeaky ball peeks from the visitor's bag while their hands are full | **Social detective inferencing**: reading body language & "full hands" clues |
| **11** | Broom-Tail Wag & Room for Both | Jovi sits for 2 wiggly seconds so the visitor can set the bag down & play fetch | How a brief wait creates space for *both* connection and fun (`First / Then`) |
| **12** | Bedtime: Piggy's Turn to Practice | Piggy tumbles off the bed; Jovi stops mid-pounce and coaches Piggy | Narrative retell, role reversal, and self-monitoring mastery |
